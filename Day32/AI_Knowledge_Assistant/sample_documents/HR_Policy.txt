ABC TECHNOLOGIES - HR POLICY

1. Casual Leave

Every employee is entitled to 12 casual leaves
per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per year.

3. Earned Leave

Employees receive 18 earned leaves per year.

4. Working Hours

Office working hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM,
Monday to Friday.

5. Work From Home

Employees can work from home two days per week
with manager approval.

6. Probation Period

The probation period for new employees is six months.

7. Notice Period

The notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days.

8. Health Insurance

The company provides health insurance coverage
of Rs. 5,00,000 per employee.

9. Training Policy

Employees can claim up to Rs. 20,000 per year
for approved professional certifications.

10. Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are provided 26 weeks
of maternity leave.
