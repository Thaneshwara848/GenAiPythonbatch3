Answer: Document Loading is used to extract textual content from external files such as PDF, DOCX and TXT.
 This extracted text is then divided into chunks, converted into embeddings, and indexed in a vector database for retrieval.
Remember: Document Loading converts files into usable text. It does not create embeddings or train the LLM.
