prompt :A prompt is an 
				instruction, question, or request given to an AI model 
				(such as ChatGPT) to generate a response.

				Prompt (Input) → AI Model (Processing) → Response (Output)

				A good prompt usually contains four elements: Role + Context + Task + Expected Output.

Prompting Type	One-Line Explanation
		1. Zero-Shot Prompting	No examples provided → AI generates a response.
		2. One-Shot Prompting	Exactly one example provided → AI follows the example.
		3. Few-Shot Prompting	Multiple examples provided → AI follows the pattern.	
		4. Role-Based Prompting	Assign a specific role → AI responds according to that role.
		5. Chain-of-Thought (CoT)	Ask AI to reason through intermediate steps → AI solves a problem logically.
		6. Constraint-Based Prompting	Specify rules or limitations → AI generates a response within those constraints.
		7. Step-by-Step Prompting	Ask AI to explain a task in ordered steps → AI provides a sequential explanation.
		8. Instruction-Based Prompting	Give clear instructions → AI performs the requested task.
		9. Output-Based Prompting	Specify the output format → AI responds in JSON, table, list, etc.	
		10. Negative Prompting	Specify what AI should avoid → AI excludes unwanted content.

Temperature in LLM: A parameter that controls the randomness and creativity of AI-generated responses.		: 
	Lower temperature produces more predictable outputs
	Higher temperature increases variety and creativity.
	
	Low temperature (0–0.2): More predictable and consistent answers.
	Medium temperature (0.3–0.6): Balance between consistency and creativity.
	High temperature (0.7–1.0): More varied and creative answers.

	Temperature	Behaviour				Example Use
	0.0 – 0.2	More consistent and predictable		Coding, factual Q&A, technical answers
	0.3 – 0.5	Balanced and focused			Chatbots, explanations
	0.6 – 0.8	More creative and varied		Content writing, storytelling
	0.9 – 1.0	Highly random and creative		Brainstorming, innovative ideas


	1. Temperature selection guide
	Temperature	When to use				Real-world example
	0.0–0.2		High consistency			RAG, invoice extraction, login test regression
	0.3–0.4		Consistency with slight variation	Functional test-case generation, code explanation
	0.5–0.6		Balanced variation			Chatbots, training explanations
	0.7–0.8		Creative responses			Marketing emails, storytelling
	0.9–1.0		Broad idea exploration			Brainstorming, creative writing

Hallucination  : In simple terms: AI gives a confident answer, but the answer is wrong.

Hallucination occurs when a Large Language Model (LLM) generates 
	incorrect, misleading, or completely fabricated 
	information and presents it as if it were true.




1. Personal Information
	Prompt: "What is Thanesh's height?"
	Hallucinated Answer
		"Thanesh's height is 5 feet 9 inches."
	Correct Answer
		"I don't know Thanesh's height because that information hasn't been provided."

	Reason: AI invented personal information.

2. Future Prediction
	Prompt: "Who will be India's Prime Minister in 2029?"
	
	Hallucinated Answer
		"Narendra Modi will definitely be the Prime Minister in 2029."
	Correct Answer
	"The result is unknown. It depends on future political developments and the electoral outcome."


Reason: AI presented an uncertain future event as an established fact.
