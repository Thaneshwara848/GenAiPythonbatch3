FICTIONAL CANCELLATION POLICY | Version 1 | Effective 2026-01-01
A confirmed booking cancelled at least 7 days before departure receives a full refund. Cancellation 2 through 6 days before departure receives a 50 percent refund. Cancellation less than 2 days before departure receives no refund. Refunds are simulated; no money is transferred. Past departures cannot be cancelled.
